    - heading "What is Cold Pressed Juice?" [level=1]
    - paragraph: We all know the delicious taste of a fresh fruit & vegetable juice made right in front of you. But what is cold pressed juice & how does that differ fromyour juices made at home?
    - paragraph: The term “cold pressed” has nothing to do with the actual temperature of the juice. “Cold pressed” refers to the process in which the fruits & vegetables are used to make juice. Cold pressed juice is made with a special type of juicer that pushes fresh produce through a blade, mashes it into pulp, & then collects the pulp into a cheesecloth-like bag. A hydraulic press then extracts the juice from the pulp while being filtered through the cheesecloth-like bag.
    - paragraph: Since the cold pressed juicer presses the produce to extract the juice, there is NO HEAT used in the entire process! Cold pressed juice protects & preserves the nutrients while giving the juices a shelf life of 3-4 days. That means you still get ALL the healthy benefits of the fruits & veggies. You get 100% of the vitamins, minerals, enzymes, & nutrients when you drink COLD PRESSED JUICE!
