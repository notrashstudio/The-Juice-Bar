# Media provenance

All catalog photography comes from the client's public Shopify product catalog. Original remote URLs and local optimized filenames are in asset-map.json. Product artwork and branding belong to the Juice Bar Company.

The transparent homepage composition was created with built-in ImageGen using the client's Green Supreme, Sunny Day, and Beet It single-bottle photographs. Prompt: isolate the three actual bottles on a transparent background, Green Supreme tilted left, Sunny Day upright centrally, Beet It tilted right; retain packaging, labels, natural colors, and readable product names. A second edit removed remaining background/glow while preserving the bottles and transparent background. The result is a creative product composition, not a photograph of a physical display.
