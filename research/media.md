The transparent homepage composition was created with built-in ImageGen using the client's Green Supreme, Sunny Day, and Beet It single-bottle photographs. Prompt: isolate the three actual bottles on a transparent background, Green Supreme tilted left, Sunny Day upright centrally, Beet It tilted right; retain packaging, labels, natural colors, and readable product names. A second edit removed remaining background/glow while preserving the bottles and transparent background. The result is a creative product composition, not a photograph of a physical display.

## October 13 product photography pass

Twenty refreshed product photographs were produced with the built-in ImageGen editor using the client's actual catalog photographs as edit targets. Each was inspected for visible product identity, labels, packaging, and package counts. The gift card remains a crisp code-native SVG and the approved transparent hero composition remains unchanged.

Final storefront files are in ../dist/assets/studio/ (one JPG per product handle). The exact final prompt set, source filenames, generated original paths and final workspace filenames are in studio-photos.json. This includes all ten juices, three shots, three jugs, sea moss, and the three cleanse packages. Images use consistent soft pastel studio backgrounds and improved lighting/clarity; the actual shot cups and unlabelled jugs remain faithful to their original packaging. JPEG conversion is format optimization only; no code-based retouching or background editing was used.

These are edited advertising visuals rather than new photographs of a physical studio setup. The underlying original photos are preserved in assets and the research audit. Old photos are no longer mixed into the current storefront gallery. Client label-content inconsistencies present in the original catalog remain preserved; no new ingredient or nutrition claim was created in the image editing pass.
